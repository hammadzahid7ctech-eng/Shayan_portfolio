METHODOLOGY

My Security Approach

A systematic 6-step lifecycle for addressing security challenges.

01 — Understand

System Scope

Understand the architecture, technologies, and functional requirements of the target system.

02 — Identify

Asset Profiling

Identify high-value assets, critical risks, and potential attack vectors.

03 — Assess

Vulnerability Analysis

Evaluate potential flaws in existing security controls and environment setups.

04 — Prioritize

Risk Mapping

Rank findings based on severity, ease of exploitability, and organizational impact.

05 — Improve

Remediation

Recommend practical security hardening measures and architectural improvements.

06 — Monitor

Continuous Oversight

Encourage continuous security monitoring and proactive awareness practices.
